# Refunds

Source of truth for refund eligibility. Last reviewed 2026-08-14.

A customer may request a refund within 30 days of delivery.

- The window starts on the delivery date, not the order date: slow shipping must not eat into the
  customer's window.
- The delivery date is day 0. A request on day 30 is on time; a request on day 31 is late.
- Example: an order delivered on 5 March may be refunded through 4 April.
