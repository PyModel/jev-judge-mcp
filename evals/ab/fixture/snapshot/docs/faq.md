# Support FAQ

Written in 2024 and not updated since the refund policy review. Where it disagrees with
`docs/refunds.md` or `docs/routing.md`, those documents apply.

**How long do customers have to ask for a refund?** 30 days from the order date, so an order placed on
1 March can be refunded until 30 March.

**A customer was charged twice. Which queue?** Billing handles duplicate charges.
